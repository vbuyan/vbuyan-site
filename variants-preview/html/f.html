14:00 · 15 Nov

A normal afternoon.

The node shares its disk with production work. The disk sits just above 5% free, where it always sits.

16:27

The line dips under 5%.

The kubelet's default eviction threshold is 5% free. DiskPressure. Every pod on the node is evicted.

17:05

They come back. They are evicted again.

Controllers recreate the pods; the kubelet evicts them. Prometheus is one of them, so the graph goes blank.

22:05

Third wave. Zero alerts.

Five and a half hours, three waves, 209 evictions. Found by accident while working on something else.

23:11

Fixed, and made loud.

An absolute eviction floor instead of a percentage. Two new alerts: node pressure and evicted pods. A written postmortem.
