## Short

DevOps and Site Reliability Engineer. Builds Kubernetes platforms that stay up, explain themselves, and
share expensive hardware fairly.

## Medium

Vitalii Buyan is a DevOps and Site Reliability Engineer with 3+ years of running Kubernetes and AWS
infrastructure in financial services: Terraform, Helm, GitHub Actions, Vault, Packer and
Prometheus/Grafana monitoring. He built [gpu-platform](/projects/gpu-platform/), an open source Kubernetes
platform that shares one GPU between teams through a fair queue, documented with 13 architecture decision
records and 3 blameless [postmortems](/postmortems/). He is studying Cloud and Network Engineering at
Western Governors University.

## What I care about

- **Reliability you can see.** Alerts for failure modes, not just for the last incident.
- **Decisions you can read.** Every non-obvious choice gets a short written record.
- **Postmortems that change something.** A default, an alert or a design, every time.
- **Fair sharing of expensive hardware.** GPUs are the most expensive thing in most clusters now.
